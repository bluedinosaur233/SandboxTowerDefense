Design reference sheet for a polished Chinese fantasy voxel tower defense game called 暮河守望. Landscape 1536x1024. Two distinct readable end-of-battle screen mockups side by side over a softly blurred green voxel fortress battlefield. Left: VICTORY, ornate aged golden brass and carved dark walnut banner with ivory parchment inset, heraldic wings, warm radiant sunbeams, three large distinct beveled five-point stars in a curved row, spacious concise numbers and two bottom action buttons. One small alternate inset shows the exact same stars transformed into azure blue bodies with a soft rose-pink gradient on the tips, luminous magical enamel rather than a gold outline. Right: DEFEAT, fractured dark iron and faded wine-red banners, broken sword crest, ash motes, charcoal parchment, solemn but readable concise stats and retry and map buttons. Use elegant Chinese display typography only for major victory/defeat titles (远征胜利 / 防线失守). Avoid crowns, modern glassmorphism, dense text, mobile ads, overly busy details, comic lettering. Hero not pictured. The design should fit a cozy epic swords and magic strategy game, stylized handpainted game UI with strong silhouettes, high text contrast, clear hierarchy and ample room for independently animated stars. This is a production art direction reference, not a flat website.
