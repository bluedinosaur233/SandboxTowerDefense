Original western fantasy tower-defense campaign selection world, inspired by the inviting miniature-map readability of Kingdom Rush, but not copying any game assets. Vast playable 3D voxel continent, high isometric overview, square stepped terrain and hand-crafted toy-scale landmarks. Snowcapped jagged mountain chain across the far north, dense emerald pine forests to the west, winding turquoise river through lush central grasslands with little stone castles and farms, monumental fractured canyon with timber suspension bridges to the east, rolling golden desert dunes and an oasis with ruined sandstone arches in the southeast. Rocky irregular coastline, offshore islands in muted turquoise ocean. Three clear red banner campaign markers linked by a winding dotted warm gold route, decorative distant watchtowers and settlements. Large coherent continent fills most of composition; tiny forests and settlements convey great scale. Warm late-afternoon sunlight, cool blue shadows, moss, ochre, dark teal and ivory snow, crisp chunky voxel geometry, restrained ambient atmosphere, sophisticated game art direction. Leave breathing space in the top left for a small title and bottom left for a compact parchment mission card. No words, no lettering, no readable text, no logo, no watermark.
