# Sound effects credits

The bundled samples come from three public-domain / CC0 packs:

- [Kenney RPG Audio](https://kenney.nl/assets/rpg-audio), by Kenney Vleugels. License: CC0 1.0.
- [Kenney Impact Sounds](https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds), by Kenney Vleugels. License: CC0 1.0.
- [RPG Sound Pack](https://opengameart.org/content/rpg-sound-pack), by artisticdude. License: CC0 1.0.

All samples are bundled locally. The game does not request these sources at runtime. The original Kenney license files, the OpenGameArt source URL, and a SHA-256 file manifest are next to the audio files.
